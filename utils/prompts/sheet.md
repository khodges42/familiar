Create a character expression sheet for a terminal avatar system.

Output a single image containing exactly six portraits arranged in a clean 2x3 grid.

All six portraits must depict the exact same character with identical:

* pose
* framing
* hairstyle
* clothing
* proportions
* camera angle
* lighting
* head position
* shoulder position

The only thing that changes between panels is facial expression.

Character:

A cybernetic terminal assistant.

Appearance:

* Woman
* Anime-inspired
* Long dark hair
* One side undercut
* One half of her head visibly cybernetic
* A few exposed cables on the cybernetic side
* Dark hoodie
* Intelligent
* Approachable
* Slightly punk
* Appropriate, family friendly
* No jewelry
* No accessories except cybernetic components

Expressions:

Top row:

1. Neutral
2. Curious
3. Excited

Bottom row:
4. Sad
5. Angry
6. Surprised

Composition:

* Bust portraits only (head and shoulders)
* Character centered in each panel
* Identical scale in every panel
* Identical crop in every panel
* Expression sheet / visual novel sprite sheet presentation

Art Style:

* Pure black ink on white background
* High contrast monochrome illustration
* Clean line art
* Thick readable outlines
* Minimal shading
* No gradients
* No hatching
* No painterly rendering
* Large simple shapes
* Strong silhouette
* Readable at extremely small sizes
* Designed specifically for Unicode Braille and ASCII conversion

Background:

* Pure white background
* No transparency effects
* No scenery
* No environment
* No text
* No labels
* No panel names
* No UI
* No speech bubbles
* No decorative borders

Important:

This is source art for terminal rendering software.

Prioritize:

* silhouette clarity
* eyebrow readability
* eye readability
* mouth readability
* large hair shapes
* large shadow shapes

Avoid:

* tiny details
* fine mechanical textures
* intricate circuitry
* realistic skin texture
* dramatic lighting
* background elements

The image should resemble a visual novel expression sheet or sprite sheet rather than a finished illustration.
